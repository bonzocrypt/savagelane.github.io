Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Savage Lane collects, uses, and protects information submitted through this website. Savage Lane is Dan Belliveau's digital product portfolio and project inquiry website.

Information collected

Savage Lane may collect information you choose to provide when you contact the site owner, request project help, or send a project inquiry.

  • Contact information such as your name, email address, business name, website, or phone number if you choose to provide it.
  • Project information such as details about a website, app, automation workflow, SEO issue, admin dashboard, or business system you want to discuss.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device information, referring pages, approximate location, IP address, and site usage data that may be collected for security, analytics, and site performance.

How information is used

Information submitted through Savage Lane may be used to:

  • Respond to project inquiries or contact requests.
  • Evaluate whether a requested website, app, dashboard, SEO cleanup, or automation project is a good fit.
  • Communicate about possible services, project scope, timelines, or next steps.
  • Improve site performance, security, content, and user experience.
  • Maintain records related to communications and project inquiries.

Analytics and technical data

Savage Lane may use analytics, hosting logs, or similar tools to understand traffic, diagnose technical issues, improve content, and protect the website from misuse. These tools may collect technical data such as device type, browser type, pages viewed, referring URLs, approximate location, and IP address.

AI assisted tools and project review

Savage Lane may use AI assisted tools, research tools, or development tools to help review project details, draft responses, plan technical work, or improve website and app builds. Do not submit confidential, regulated, or sensitive information unless it is necessary for the project discussion and you are comfortable sharing it.

Sharing information

Savage Lane does not sell personal information. Information may be shared only when reasonably needed to operate the website, respond to a request, provide requested services, use trusted service providers, comply with law, protect rights and security, or complete work you have requested.

Service providers

Savage Lane may rely on third party services for hosting, analytics, email, forms, development tools, security, search visibility, or workflow support. These providers may process information only as needed to provide their services.

Data retention

Information may be kept as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, evaluate projects, maintain business records, improve the site, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations.

Your choices

  • You can choose not to submit personal information through the website.
  • You can request access, correction, or deletion of information you previously submitted, subject to legal, security, and business recordkeeping limits.
  • You can ask not to receive further communication related to an inquiry.

Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information submitted through Savage Lane. No website, email system, hosting platform, or data transmission method can be guaranteed to be fully secure.

External links

Savage Lane links to portfolio projects, app listings, and other external websites. Those websites have their own privacy practices, and Savage Lane is not responsible for their content, security, or privacy policies.

Children's privacy

Savage Lane is not intended for children under 13. The website is intended for business, portfolio, and professional project inquiry purposes.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date above will reflect the latest version.

Contact
