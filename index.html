Why Savage Lane is different

A useful product is not just a homepage. It is the structure behind the screen: the data model, user flow, backend logic, admin process, SEO foundation, permissions, form handling, and edge case cleanup.

Before building Savage Lane projects, Dan spent more than 17 years at Fidelity Investments across trading, Active Trader services, platform product development, equities, options, margin, client support, compliance driven operations, and trading technology.

That background creates a different kind of builder mindset. Speed matters. Accuracy matters. Workflow design matters. Risk awareness matters. Products need to be clean enough for users and structured enough for the business behind them.

Define the job

Clarify the business goal, user flow, public message, private workflow, and what the product must prove.

Build the system

Create the frontend, backend, data model, forms, storage, dashboards, SEO structure, and deployment path.

Improve after launch

Use real use, search data, feedback, and production problems to make the product cleaner and more useful.
