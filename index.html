Level 1 cleared · About

Hi, I’m Dan Belliveau.

From the trading desk to building digital products.

Today, I serve as CEO, CFO, and CTO of Vaulted Holdings LLC, bringing business strategy, financial oversight, and software development together. I take digital ideas from concept through launch, designing user experiences, building applications, and developing the infrastructure behind them—including user accounts, databases, payments, analytics, and automation.

Before that, I spent more than 17 years in Fidelity Investments’ active trading segment in Boston, Massachusetts, and Merrimack, New Hampshire. My career progressed from customer service for active traders to trading-platform product development and a senior role on the Active Trader Desk. I worked with complex options traders, reconciled margin accounts, oversaw trading representatives, approved high-net-worth trading activity, supervised trading risk, audited departmental activity, reviewed client calls, and coached trading representatives. In product development, I wrote specifications for platform components and coordinated teams to bring trading-platform improvements into production.

That combination of trading experience and technical development carries into my work today. I’ve developed automated trading scripts involving live market data, order execution, position reconciliation, and safeguards such as loss limits and restart protection. My experience also includes blockchain technology and creating and releasing my own altcoins.

After moving to Florida, I broadened my experience through general management in the food and beverage industry, hands-on installation of smart-home and IoT technology, and residential real estate. Those roles added practical experience in running a business, troubleshooting technology, and helping people navigate significant decisions.

I maintain my Florida real estate license and now work on a referral basis, connecting buyers and sellers with agents worldwide. If you’re considering buying or selling a home, speak with me before choosing an agent or taking your first steps—I can help you find the right connection.
